How Does the Final Grade Calculator Work?

The Final Grade Calculator uses your current course grade, desired overall grade, and final exam weight to determine the score you need on your final exam.

The Final Grade Formula

Required Exam Grade = (Desired Grade − Current Grade × (1 − Exam Weight)) ÷ Exam Weight

The exam weight is converted from a percentage into a decimal before the calculation is performed.

Example

Suppose your current grade is 85%, you want an 88% final grade, and your final exam is worth 30% of the course.

Enter:

  • Current Grade: 85%
  • Desired Grade: 88%
  • Final Exam Weight: 30%

The calculator will determine the exact exam score required to reach your target.

Can I Get a 100% on My Final Exam?

If the calculator shows a required grade above 100%, the desired overall course grade cannot be achieved through the final exam alone. The calculator also considers the maximum possible overall grade when the final exam score is 100%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate what grade I need on my final?

Enter your current grade, desired final grade, and the percentage of your course grade represented by the final exam. The calculator will calculate your required exam score.

What is the formula for calculating my required final grade?

The formula is: Required Exam Grade = (Desired Grade − Current Grade × (1 − Exam Weight)) ÷ Exam Weight.

What if I need more than 100% on my final?

A required score above 100% means your desired overall grade is not mathematically achievable through the final exam alone.

Can I use this calculator for college or high school?

Yes. The calculator can be used for most courses that use percentage-based grades and a weighted final exam.
