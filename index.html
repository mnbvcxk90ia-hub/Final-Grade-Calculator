How Does the Final Grade Calculator Work?

Our Final Grade Calculator helps students determine what score they need on their final exam to achieve their desired overall course grade.

1. Enter Your Current Grade

Enter the grade you currently have in your course before taking the final exam.

2. Enter Your Desired Grade

Enter the overall course grade you want to achieve.

3. Enter the Final Exam Weight

Enter the percentage of your course grade that comes from the final exam.

4. Calculate

The calculator will show the exact percentage you need to score on your final exam.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Final Grade Calculator?

A Final Grade Calculator calculates the exam score required to reach a desired overall course grade.

What if I need more than 100%?

If the required grade is above 100%, your desired final course grade cannot be reached with the current grades and exam weight you entered.
